## 5. How to sign, and what is recorded

Signing happens once, in the pull request: a bot asks any contributor who has
not signed yet, and You sign by posting the exact sentence

> I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA

as a comment on the pull request. Your signature is recorded as a JSON entry
(GitHub login and user id, the pull request number, the date, and the version
of this agreement) on the `cla-signatures` branch of the Project's public
repository, where anyone can see it. One signature covers all Your later
Contributions. If this agreement changes in substance, a new version is
published and the bot asks again.
